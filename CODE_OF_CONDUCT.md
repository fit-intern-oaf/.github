# Code of Conduct

OpenAIFlex adopts the **Contributor Covenant v2.1** verbatim.

Full text: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

## Reporting

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the project leads at
`openaiflex-conduct@fit.fraunhofer.de` (alias set up at M1 — TODO).

All complaints are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
Project leads are obligated to respect the privacy and security of reporters.

## Enforcement

Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant 2.1 Community Impact Guidelines.
Decisions are made by the OpenAIFlex Steering Committee representative at
Fraunhofer FIT, in consultation with the project coordinator (INESC TEC) for
cross-partner incidents.
